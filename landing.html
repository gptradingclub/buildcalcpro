FAQ

Common Questions.

Is there a free trial?

No free trial right now — but if BuildCalc Pro doesn't save you time on your first estimate, just cancel within 7 days and email gptradingclub@gmail.com for a full refund. No questions asked.

How is the AI Market Check different from just Googling prices?

Our AI searches reputable contractor sources (HomeAdvisor, Angi, Fixr, Forbes Home), cross-references them against your specific specs (region, material grade, project size), and gives you a verdict: LOW / ON MARKET / HIGH with % difference. It also flags current market factors like supply chain issues or seasonal pricing.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from your Stripe customer portal with one click. No contracts, no cancellation fees, no questions. You'll keep access until the end of your current billing period.

What if I work in multiple regions?

Switch regions per estimate. Northeast for jobs in CT, Florida for jobs in FL — labor and material costs auto-adjust based on real regional multipliers (Northeast labor is 1.35x Midwest baseline, for example).

Does it work on mobile?

Yes — works in any modern browser on phone, tablet, or laptop. No app download needed. Generate estimates on-site with your phone.

How accurate are the base prices?

Base prices are calibrated for Midwest USA (national average), then adjusted regionally. The AI Live Market Check verifies against current market data on each estimate. Prices are refreshed monthly. We've calibrated against thousands of real contractor quotes.

Will the price stay $39/mo?

Early adopters get locked-in pricing. If you sign up at $39/mo today, your rate stays $39/mo forever — even when we raise prices for new customers.
